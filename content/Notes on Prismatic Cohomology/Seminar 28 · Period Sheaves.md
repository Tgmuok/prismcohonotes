Let $X$ be a rigid analytic space over a $p$-adic field $K$. Let $C = \widehat{\overline{K}}$.

**Remark 28.1.** We will consider the restriction of sheaves on $X$ to $X_C$. Note that $\widehat{\mathcal{O}}_X|_{X_C} = \widehat{\mathcal{O}}_{X_C}$, but $\mathcal{O}_X|_{X_C} \neq \mathcal{O}_{X_C}$. The sheaf $\mathcal{O}_X|_{X_C}$ is a sheaf of $\overline{K}$-vector spaces, while the others are sheaves of $C$-vector spaces.

We want to prove a version of the Poincaré lemma on the quasi-pro-étale site. To do this, we need to introduce some new sheaves. Let $\mathbb{A}_{\mathrm{inf}}$ be the sheaf on $X_{\mathrm{qproét}}$ sending $\mathrm{Spa}(R, R^+)$ to $W(R^+)$. Let

$$
\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{inf}} = \mathbb{A}_{\mathrm{inf}} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_p} \mathbb{Q}_p,
$$

There are maps $\Theta: \mathbb{A}_{\mathrm{inf}} \to \widehat{\mathcal{O}}_X^+$, $\Theta_{\mathbb{Q}}: \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{inf}} \to \widehat{\mathcal{O}}_X$. Let

$$
\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ = \varprojlim_n \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{inf}} / (\ker \Theta_{\mathbb{Q}})^n.
$$

>[!abstract]+ Prop 28.2
>
>Locally on $X_{\mathrm{qproét}}$, the ideal sheaf $\ker \Theta_{\mathbb{Q}}$ is principal and generated by a non-zero-divisor.

Then we define $\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}} = \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ (\ker \Theta_{\mathbb{Q}})^{-1}$.

>[!abstract]+ Prop 28.3
>
>If $Y$ is affinoid perfectoid, then $H^i(Y, \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+) = 0$ and $H^i(Y, \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}) = 0$ for $i > 0$.

The primitive comparison theorem has the following consequences:

>[!abstract]+ Prop 28.4
>
>$$
>H_{\mathrm{ét}}^i(X_C, \mathbb{Z}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_p} \mathbb{A}_{\mathrm{inf}}^a \cong H^i(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \mathbb{A}_{\mathrm{inf}}^a)
>$$
>$$
>H_{\mathrm{ét}}^i(X_C, \mathbb{Q}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}_p} \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{inf}}^a \cong H^i(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{inf}}^a)
>$$
>$$
>H_{\mathrm{ét}}^i(X_C, \mathbb{Q}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}_p} B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \cong H^i(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+)
>$$
>$$
>H_{\mathrm{ét}}^i(X_C, \mathbb{Q}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}_p} B_{\mathrm{dR}} \cong H^i(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}})
>$$
>
>In the first two isomorphisms, we consider a module to be almost zero if it is killed by $[\mathfrak{m}_{C^\flat}]$.

>[!notes]+ Def 28.5
>
>Let $\mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$ be the sheafification of the presheaf that sends an affinoid perfectoid object $\mathrm{Spa}(R, R^+)$ satisfying the conditions of the previous lemma to
>$$
>\varinjlim_i \varprojlim_n \left( (R_i^+ \widehat{\otimes}_{K_0} \mathbb{A}_{\mathrm{inf}}(R, R^+)) [1/p] \right) / (\ker \Theta)^n.
>$$
>
>This definition does not depend on the choice of the system $(R_i, R_i^+)$.
>
>Let $\mathrm{Fil}^n \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ = (\ker \Theta)^n \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$.
>
>Let $\mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}} = \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \otimes_{\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+} \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}$.
>
>There are maps
>$$
>\mathcal{O}_X \hookrightarrow \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \to \widehat{\mathcal{O}}_X.
>$$

>[!tip]+ lemma 28.6
>
>Let $\mathbb{T} = \mathrm{Spa}\, K \langle T^{\pm 1} \rangle$, and let $\mathbb{T}_\infty = \mathrm{Spa}\, C \langle T^{\pm 1/p^\infty} \rangle$. Let $U$ be an open subspace of $X$. Suppose we are given an étale map $U \to \mathbb{T}$ that factors as a composite of rational embeddings and finite étale maps. Let $\tilde{X} = X \times_\mathbb{T} \mathbb{T}_\infty$. Then the map
>$$
>\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+|_{\tilde{X}} \left [ \left [ X_1, \dots, X_n  \right ]  \right ]  \to \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+|_{\tilde{X}}
>$$
>sending $X_i \mapsto T_i \otimes 1 - 1 \otimes [T_i^\flat]$ is an isomorphism.

For each $i$, let $\Omega_X^i$ be the sheaf on $X_{\mathrm{proét}}$ defined by $\Omega_X^n = \nu^* \Omega_{X_{\mathrm{ét}}}^i$. The sheaf $\mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$ admits a unique $\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$-linear connection

$$
\nabla: \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \to \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \Omega_X^1,
$$

and similarly, there is a unique $\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}$-linear connection

$$
\nabla: \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}} \to \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \Omega_X^1.
$$

>[!danger]+ Them 28.7 (Poincaré lemma)
>
>The sequence
>$$
>0 \to \mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \to \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \xrightarrow{\nabla} \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \Omega_X^1 \xrightarrow{\nabla} \dots \xrightarrow{\nabla} \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}^+ \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \Omega_X^n \to 0
>$$
>is exact.
>
>>*Proof.* This follows from Lemma 28.6.

>[!done]+ Coro 28.8
>
>$$
>H_{\mathrm{ét}}^i(X_C, \mathbb{Q}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}_p} B_{\mathrm{dR}} \cong H^i(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \Omega_X^\bullet)
>$$
>
>>*Proof.* Combine Proposition 28.4 and Theorem 28.7.

>[!tip]+ lemma 28.9
>
>For each $i$, we have
>$$
>\mathrm{gr}^i \, \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}|_{\tilde{X}} \cong \xi^i \widehat{\mathcal{O}}_X \left[ \frac{X_1}{\xi}, \dots, \frac{X_n}{\xi} \right],
>$$
>where $\xi$ is any generator of $\ker \Theta$.

>[!tip]+ lemma 28.10
>
>Let $U = \mathrm{Spa}(A, A^+)$ be an open affinoid subspace of $X$ admitting an étale map $U \to \mathbb{T}$ that factors as a composite of rational embeddings and finite étale maps. Then
>$$
>H^i(U_C, \mathrm{gr}^0 \, \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}}) =
>\begin{cases}
>A \hat{\otimes}_K C, & i = 0 \\
>0, & i > 0.
>\end{cases}
>$$
>
>>*Proof.* Combine Lemma 28.9 with a Galois cohomology calculation.

>[!done]+ Coro 28.11
>
>For any integers $i,j$, we have an isomorphism
>$$
>\mathrm{gr}^i \, B_{\mathrm{dR}} \otimes_{\overline{K}} R\Gamma(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \Omega_X^j) \cong R\Gamma(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \mathrm{gr}^i \, \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \Omega_X^j).
>$$

>[!done]+ Coro 28.12
>
>The map
>$$
>R\Gamma(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \Omega_X^\bullet) \otimes_{\overline{K}} B_{\mathrm{dR}} \to R\Gamma(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \Omega_X^\bullet \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \mathcal{O}\mathbb{B}_{\mathrm{dR}})
>$$
>is an isomorphism.

>[!tip]+ lemma 28.13
>
>$$
>R\Gamma(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \Omega_X^\bullet) \cong R\Gamma(X_{\mathrm{an}}, \Omega_X^\bullet) \otimes_K \overline{K}
>$$
>
>>*Proof.* By [Sch, Proposition 14.9],
>>$$
>>R\Gamma(X_{C,\mathrm{qproét}}, \Omega_X^\bullet) \cong \varinjlim_{L/K \text{ finite}} R\Gamma(X_{L,\mathrm{ét}}, \Omega_X^\bullet).
>>$$
>>By [FvdP04, Proposition 8.2.3]
>>$$
>>R\Gamma(X_{L,\mathrm{ét}}, \Omega_X^\bullet) \cong R\Gamma(X_{L,\mathrm{an}}, \Omega_{X_{\mathrm{an}}}^\bullet).
>>$$
>>Since $L$ is flat over $K$,
>>$$
>>R\Gamma(X_{L,\mathrm{an}}, \Omega_{X_{\mathrm{an}}}^\bullet) \cong R\Gamma(X_{\mathrm{an}}, \Omega_{X_{\mathrm{an}}}^\bullet) \otimes_K L.
>>$$
>>Now the lemma follows from the fact that direct limits commute with tensor product.

>[!danger]+ Them 28.14 (de Rham comparison)
>
>$$
>H_{\mathrm{ét}}^i(X, \mathbb{Q}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}_p} B_{\mathrm{dR}} \cong H^i(X_{\mathrm{an}}, \Omega_X^\bullet) \otimes_K B_{\mathrm{dR}}
>$$
>
>>*Proof.* Combine Corollary 28.8, Corollary 28.12, and Lemma 28.13.
